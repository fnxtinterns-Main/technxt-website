Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Project Cancellation Before Sprint Commencement

If a client requests project cancellation within 48 hours of contract signing and prior to the commencement of technical architecture or discovery design, 100% of the advance fee minus banking transaction charges will be refunded without questions.

2. Milestone-Based Development Refund Schedule

Custom software engineering is billed against verified milestones. Once an intermediate milestone is completed, demonstrated on staging, and approved by the Client, payments allocated to that completed milestone become non-refundable. For any unstarted future milestones, remaining advance funds will be refunded on a pro-rata basis.

3. Monthly Maintenance & Retainer Cancellation

Website Maintenance & SLA Retainer contracts can be terminated at any time with a 15-day written notice via email to india.futurenxt@gmail.com. Billing stops immediately at the end of the current billing cycle with zero penalty fees.

4. Non-Refundable Disbursals

Fees disbursed directly to third-party providers (such as domain registrations, third-party SSL certificates, non-transferable SaaS subscriptions, and direct AWS infrastructure usage) are non-refundable as third-party providers do not allow clawbacks.

5. Refund Processing Method & Timeline

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment channel (Bank Transfer / NEFT / RTGS / Payment Gateway) within 5 to 7 business banking days from the date of formal cancellation confirmation.
